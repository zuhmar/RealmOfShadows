    32 shared bag slots · Each supply stack or unequipped item uses one slot. Equipped gear has separate slots. Sell unwanted items to Mara.

    Professions and crafting

    Gather by clicking resource nodes; click water to fish. Visit the Crafting Hall: Bram for Blacksmithing, Elara for Woodworking, Tobin for Cooking, and Iris for Alchemy. Click a crafter or their custom bench. The public town workbench also remains available. Skills level independently; no class restrictions. Crafted equipment is identified Uncommon gear.

    Mining: Copper → Iron → Gold → Mythril → Dragon → Shadow. All ore, tree and plant varieties can appear on every map, gated by profession level. Local-level resources are more common. Land nodes refresh every five minutes; fishing takes 1–10 seconds per cast and can be recast immediately. Cooked fish restores 30 HP and shares the potion cooldown.

    Quests
