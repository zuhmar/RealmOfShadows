# Art references

Character, outfit and monster designs follow the user's supplied references.
Wings and auras also follow their supplied sheets. Generate the required game
views and animation poses from those designs while keeping their identity,
proportions, silhouettes and colors consistent.

Maps, terrain, items and other environmental assets may use generated artwork.
Preserve uploaded reference images; place derived art and runtime assets in
their corresponding asset pipelines rather than editing these originals.

- `outfits/`: the two approved outfit sheets, including male/female variants.
- `wings-and-auras/`: the approved wing and aura collections.

See [the cosmetic pipeline](../../docs/skins-and-cosmetics.md) for current
runtime artwork, extraction and saved-choice migration.
